Transistor Fine-Tuning and Tool-Use Evaluation

Research & Ideas · Research

Research evaluating vision-language models on transistor analysis and TCAD tool use. The study compares a base model with adapter fine-tuning, examines guided error analysis, and explores persistent memory as part of the workflow. Experiments use Python, PyTorch, VLMs, and TCAD; results and limitations are recorded separately so the evaluation is not presented as more conclusive than it is.

Qiskit ket_basis Visualization

Research & Ideas · Merged contribution

A merged contribution to Qiskit that adds X, Y, and Z ket-basis choices to quantum-state LaTeX visualization. The change includes tests and a release note, and was merged on October 22, 2026. It uses Python and Qiskit's quantum-computing visualization code.

Qiskit Transpiler Optimization Investigation

Research & Ideas · Investigation

An investigation into a circuit-cancellation issue in Qiskit's transpiler. After reproducing the behavior, the proposed change was compared across circuit families; benchmarking recorded both gate reductions and the runtime overhead. This remains an investigation and is not represented as a merged contribution.

Minecraft Redstone Qubit Simulator

Research & Ideas · One-qubit build

A Minecraft Java world that models a classical one-qubit stabilizer workflow using redstone logic. The build demonstrates X, Z, Hadamard, and measurement operations and is available to download from the repository. Two-qubit entanglement is a planned extension, not part of the current build.

Matmul-Optimized Accelerator

Research & Ideas · Concept study

A computer-architecture concept study about accelerating matrix multiplication. It examines systolic arrays, tiling, memory bandwidth, and data movement as design considerations; RTL and FPGA implementation are proposed next steps, not completed deliverables.
